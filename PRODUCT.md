# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Prospective students & their parents** — high-achieving high schoolers already admitted to Ohio State Honors (Fisher College of Business or College of Engineering), deciding whether to apply to IBE. They compare programs; credibility and outcomes matter.
- **Current students** — checking recruitment timelines, events, and leadership info.
- **Alumni & corporate sponsors** — validating the program's network and placement record.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for the Integrated Business & Engineering (IBE) Honors Program at The Ohio State University. It exists to convert admitted honors students into IBE applicants, and to signal program prestige to sponsors and alumni. Success = interest-form submissions and a first impression that matches the program's outcomes (100% placement, $96k average starting salary).

## Brand Personality

Collegiate, credible, ambitious. The voice of a selective honors program: confident without being flashy, warm without being casual. Anchored in Ohio State's visual identity — scarlet (#BA0C2F), gray (#646A6E), white — which is non-negotiable university branding.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing-page aesthetics (gradient meshes, glassmorphism, floating cards).
- Startup hype copy or trendy web3/AI visual tropes.
- Anything that drifts off OSU's scarlet/gray palette — the university brand is the identity.

## Design Principles

1. **Outcomes lead** — placement, salary, and alumni companies are the strongest proof; give them stage presence.
2. **University-grade polish** — the site should feel like it belongs to a top-20 public university, not a student side project.
3. **Photography carries warmth** — real cohort photos over illustration; the community is the product.
4. **Scarlet is the voice, white is the stage** — committed scarlet bands for emphasis, generous white space everywhere else.
5. **Restrained motion** — subtle reveals that respect prefers-reduced-motion; never carnival.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1.
- Full keyboard navigability; visible focus states.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation.
- Semantic landmarks (header/main/footer) and a logical heading hierarchy per page.
